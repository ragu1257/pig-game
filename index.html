Welcome to the Pig Game!

The objective of the game is to be the first player to score 50 points. Players take turns rolling a dice and accumulate the result in their current score. If a player rolls a 1, their turn ends and they lose their accumulated score. Players can choose to "Hold" and add their current score to their total score, but they risk losing their turn if they roll a 1.
